Every ratio you need, in one place

Each calculator explains what the number means before it asks you to compute it — so you're not just plugging in figures blind.

P/E Ratio

Are you paying a fair price for what the company earns?

Price ÷ Earnings per Share

Current Ratio

Can the company cover its bills due within a year?

Current Assets ÷ Current Liabilities

ROE

How efficiently is your invested capital being used?

Net Income ÷ Shareholder Equity

XIRR

What did your actual investments return, given irregular deposits?

Annualized rate across dated cash flows

YTM

What will a bond actually yield if held to maturity?

Total return incl. coupons + price change

Risk-Reward Ratio

Is this trade worth the downside you're risking?

Potential Profit ÷ Potential Loss
